about the sounds

built-in sounds are generated in the browser: no download, no network, and they keep playing offline and with the screen off. each has a tone control instead of speed. YouTube layers bring anything else, with speed and a full-screen visualiser, but need a connection, and they stream and decode video even though only the audio is used, so several at once will warm a phone and drain it. video is held at the lowest quality unless you open the visualiser; on battery, prefer the built-in sounds. add this to your phone's home screen and it runs as its own app. audio begins after your first tap. speed steps in quarters, which is the finest YouTube's player allows. mixing several videos works best with the screen on; turn screen staying on on if Android pauses background audio. expand any video row and tap visualise to fill the screen with it. if playback stutters, switch the visualiser to fit: it letterboxes instead of cropping, which is far lighter on the phone. your current blend is remembered between visits.

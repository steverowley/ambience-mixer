Built-in sounds are generated in the browser — no download, no network, and they keep playing offline and with the screen off. Each has a Tone control instead of Speed. YouTube layers bring anything else, with speed and a full-screen visualiser, but need a connection. Add this to your phone's home screen and it runs as its own app. Audio begins after your first tap. Speed steps in 0.25× increments — that's the finest YouTube's player allows. Mixing several videos works best with the screen on; turn on Screen staying on in the bar below if Android pauses background audio. Tap Visualise on any video layer to fill the screen with it. If playback stutters, switch the visualiser to Fit — it letterboxes instead of cropping, which is far lighter on the phone. A preset replaces what's playing; save your own blend first if you want to keep it. Your current blend is remembered between visits.
